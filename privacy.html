Privacy Policy – Kodak Photo Sync

Kodak Photo Sync is a personal script used only by its owner.

  • It uploads photos and videos from the owner's own camera to the owner's own Google Photos account.
  • The Google Photos access it is granted is used only to create one album and add those uploads to it.
  • Sign-in tokens and a list of uploaded files are stored only on the owner's computer.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with any third party.
